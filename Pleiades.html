Data are from The Seven Sisters DANCe by Bouy et al. (2015).

Tycho and DANCe catalog files have been merged, limited to stars with 95 percent probability of membership, and stored in a cvs file in github to avoid outages due to CDS downtime.

Many thanks to Bouy et al for the data, and Dr. Larson for the web interface!
